The Beginning

How It All Started

In the summer of 1985, Smt. Indira Sharma sat in her blue-walled courtyard in Jodhpur's old city, carefully stirring raw mango into a clay pot filled with mustard oil, fenugreek seeds, and her mother's cherished spice blend. It was not a business plan — it was muscle memory passed through three generations.

Word spread through the mohalla. Neighbours began dropping off empty jars. Local shops asked to stock them. Within three years, what started as a household ritual became a small-batch artisan enterprise — run entirely by Indira and her daughters in that same courtyard.

Today, her son Vikram Sharma leads Achaar House, shipping across India while fiercely protecting the original processes: the same cold-pressed mustard oil, the same 21-day sun-curing, the same clay-pot ageing. "We can grow," says Vikram, "but we can never rush the sun."

Our Journey

38 Years of Growing

1985
Born in a Courtyard
Smt. Indira Sharma makes her first batch of Classic Mango in Jodhpur's old city. Neighbours soon start placing orders.
1992
First Shop in Jodhpur
Achaar House opens its first physical store on Sardar Market Road, stocking 12 pickle varieties.
2003
Pan-Rajasthan Distribution
A regional distribution partnership brings Achaar House pickles to grocery stores across all 33 districts of Rajasthan.
2015
Award: Best Artisan Brand
Achaar House wins the National Food Excellence Award for Best Artisan Food Brand at the IFFT, Delhi.
2019
Online & Pan-India
Launch of the e-commerce platform. Achaar House now ships to 500+ cities across India.
2026
40+ Varieties & Growing
Vikram Sharma leads a team of 68 artisans, now serving over 38,000 households with 40+ varieties. The courtyard recipe hasn't changed.
