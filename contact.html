Frequently Asked

Common Questions

Do you ship outside India? +
Currently we ship domestically across all 500+ cities in India via reputed courier partners. We are working on international shipping for NRI customers — sign up for our newsletter to be notified when it launches!
What is the shelf life of Achaar House pickles? +
All our pickles have a shelf life of 12–18 months when stored in a cool, dry place. Once opened, refrigerate and consume within 3–4 months. Always use a clean, dry spoon to maintain freshness.
Can I place a bulk or wholesale order? +
Yes! We supply to restaurants, hotels, grocery chains, and corporate gifting clients. Wholesale orders (minimum 50 jars) receive special pricing and priority dispatch. Select "Wholesale" in our contact form above or call us directly.
Are your pickles made with artificial preservatives? +
Absolutely not. Our preservation method is entirely traditional — salt, mustard oil, acidity from the fruits/vegetables, and sun-curing. No artificial preservatives, no artificial colours, no synthetic flavours — ever.
Do you offer customised corporate or wedding hampers? +
We love custom hampers! You can choose specific pickle varieties, select packaging style (cloth bag, wooden crate, or premium gift box), and even add a personalised note card. Contact us at least 7 days in advance for custom orders.
What oil is used in Achaar House pickles? +
We exclusively use cold-pressed kachi ghani mustard oil, sourced from traditional oil mills in Rajasthan. It is the backbone of authentic North Indian achaar flavour and aroma — and has been our singular choice since 1985.
