Our Heritage

Born in a Rajasthani Kitchen

In 1985, our founder Smt. Indira Sharma began making achaar in her courtyard in Jodhpur — using her mother's handwritten recipes, cold-pressed mustard oil, and sun-kissed produce from the local sabzi mandi.

What started as jars gifted to neighbours soon became a beloved brand. Today, Achaar House ships across India, but every recipe, every spice blend, every batch still carries the soul of that courtyard kitchen.
